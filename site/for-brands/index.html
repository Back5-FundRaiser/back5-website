Community sport is huge, and almost impossible to buy.

Australia has more than 70,000 community sports clubs, kept going by around 1.8 million volunteers. Each one keeps its members in a different place, if it keeps them anywhere.

So most brand money lands as a logo and a cheque. It buys goodwill, and then nobody can say what it did. Grassroots Connector gives you one program across many clubs, with a direct and consented line to the households behind them.

What a program gives you

A way into the club

Reach the players, parents, coaches and volunteers at every participating club, through the app they already open each week.

A place in the routine

Your brand sits inside club activity and club messages, so it turns up when people are getting ready for training or a game.

Activations, run for you

Local offers, game-day campaigns and rewards, delivered club by club without your team chasing 200 committees.

Proof it worked

Reporting on engagement, participation and the outcomes we agree up front, ready to put in front of your board.

Something real for the clubs

Every program sends visible value back to the clubs and their communities. That's why they say yes, and why members pay attention.

How a club family meets your brand

  1. 1

    They open the club app

    Your brand is there from the first tap, as part of the club experience.

  2. 2

    They see something useful

    Offers and content sit beside club updates, in the place they already check.

  3. 3

    A nudge lands at the right time

    Training and game schedules tell us when a local message will be welcome.

  4. 4

    Game day brings it to life

    Activations and rewards at the ground make your brand part of the weekend.

  5. 5

    They buy, and the club benefits

    Purchases link back to their club, so spending with you helps their own team.

  6. 6

    Everyone sees the return

    The club sees what came back to it. You see what the program delivered.
